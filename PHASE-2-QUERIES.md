# Phase 2 — Query Bank (run in ChatGPT / Perplexity / Copilot)

Two jobs:
- **A. Proof-capture** — run, screenshot the answer + its citation cards, save the
  image. This is the un-fakeable "who does the AI name today" evidence per page.
- **B. Research** — run (Perplexity is best, it shows sources), then save the *link +
  the number + the date*. Every published claim needs a named, dated source.

How to save: for proof, screenshot the full answer incl. sources. For research, copy
the source URL and the exact figure into the matching page section of
`PHASE-2-QUESTIONS.md` or paste back to me. Note the date you ran it (answers drift).

Run proof queries in **all three** (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Copilot) — they cite
differently, and the divergence is itself a finding worth showing.

## B. RESEARCH QUERIES (save link + number + date)

### Cross-cutting / platform facts
- How many weekly active users does ChatGPT have as of 2026? Cite OpenAI's own
  announcement with date.
- Does ChatGPT search ground its web answers through the Bing index? Source it.
- What is the GPTBot user agent vs. OAI-SearchBot — what does each one do? Cite
  OpenAI docs.
- What is the PerplexityBot user agent and how does Perplexity's crawler behave?
  Cite Perplexity docs.
- Does Perplexity weight content freshness/recency in its citations? Any source.
- Source the Princeton GEO study claim that comparison/quotation content lifts AI
  citations (the "+115%" figure) — give the paper title, authors, year, link.

### Handwerker
- MyHammer / MyHammer Plus commission and per-lead pricing in Germany 2026 — exact
  numbers, official source.
- How many member businesses does the Handwerkskammer Hamburg have? Dated source.
- German statistic: share of consumers using AI/ChatGPT to find local tradespeople
  (Bitkom or similar), with year.

### Ärzte
- German data: % of patients (especially under 35) using AI/ChatGPT to search for
  doctors — Bitkom or a Krankenkasse study, dated.
- Heilmittelwerbegesetz (HWG): which paragraphs constrain what a medical practice
  may claim in online/advertising content? Cite the law text.
- Doctolib vs. Jameda market position in Germany 2026 — user/practice numbers, dated.

### Anwälte
- Full text and current interpretation of §43b BRAO and the Sachlichkeitsgebot on
  lawyer advertising. Cite the statute.
- anwalt.de and JUVE: evidence they function as authority/citation signals for AI.
- Typical Mandatswert (case value) ranges by Rechtsgebiet in Germany — for an ROI
  figure, with source.
- German statistic on legal-services discovery shifting to AI/online search.

### Immobilien
- Current Hamburg residential €/m² by Stadtteil (Eppendorf, HafenCity, Blankenese,
  Altona) — a dated portal or Maklerverband report.
- Maklerprovision and Bestellerprinzip rules as they stand in 2026 — who pays, what
  split, legal source.
- ImmoScout24 market share in Germany, dated.

### E-Commerce
- Schema.org Product: current required vs. recommended properties (for the table).
  Cite schema.org + Google's product structured data docs.
- Status of Perplexity Shopping and ChatGPT shopping features in 2026, dated.
- A statistic on product research starting in AI assistants vs. Google/Amazon.

### Dienstleister
- Any credible data on referral-network decay or the decline of "warm intro"
  discovery for professional services.
